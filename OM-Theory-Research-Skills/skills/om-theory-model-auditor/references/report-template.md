# Theory-model audit report

## 1. Headline verdict

State whether the main results survive, which claims fail, and whether the paper is safe to draft or circulate.

## 2. Model map

Summarize players, timing, information, decisions, objectives, constraints, equilibrium concept, and parameter domains.

When relevant, add:

| Policy | Adoption rule | Decision owner | Intensity | Instrument set | Feasibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 3. Critical issues

| Severity | Location | Issue | Why it matters | Minimal correction |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Use `critical` for an invalid headline result, `major` for an incomplete equilibrium or omitted region, and `minor` for a local error that does not change the result.

## 4. Claim ledger

| Claim | Conditions | Verdict | Evidence | Downstream impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## 5. Independent derivation

Show the shortest complete derivation needed to reproduce the equilibrium and thresholds. Include boundary comparisons and deviations.

## 6. Counterexamples and stress tests

List admissible parameter values, computed outcomes, violated claims, and reproducibility details.

## 7. Corrected results

Give publication-ready corrected statements, followed by proof or an explicit unresolved proof obligation.

## 8. Mechanism and robustness map

Separate formal drivers, economic mechanisms, managerial implications, and extension classifications.

## 9. Benchmark, assumption, and stakeholder audit

- Record whether claimed closest-paper benchmarks are exactly recovered.
- List consequential assumptions, their roles, excluded cases, nonemptiness evidence, and relaxation effects.
- Provide a stakeholder outcome ledger for every `optimal`, `preferred`, `dominates`, `necessary`, or `win-win` claim.

When information design is present, also provide:

- the state-prior-experiment-posterior-action contract;
- the preference-alignment map;
- the experiment-feasibility, Bayes-plausibility, and support-reduction verdicts;
- the informativeness-measure and discontinuity audit;
- the fixed-lever versus joint-lever comparison.

## 10. Result provenance and analytical-numerical status

| Result or finding | New/adapted/specialized/inherited | Source or analytical basis | Numerical component | Scope justified | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 11. Narrative-consistency map

| Formal result | Abstract/introduction | Main-text mechanism | Visual | Literature contrast | Conclusion | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Record missing conditions, stronger prose claims, incorrect mechanism attribution, and terminology drift.

## 12. Release decision

Choose one: `ready for drafting`, `ready after named corrections`, or `not ready`. List the exact conditions for advancing.
