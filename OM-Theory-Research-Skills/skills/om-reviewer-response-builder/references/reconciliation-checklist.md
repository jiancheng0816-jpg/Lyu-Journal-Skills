# Revision and response reconciliation checklist

Run this audit after the response letter and revised manuscript are both complete.

## Coverage

- [ ] Every editor, associate-editor, and reviewer comment has a stable identifier and a response.
- [ ] Compound comments are either answered together coherently or split without dropping a subpart.
- [ ] Cross-reviewer issues receive consistent dispositions.
- [ ] The major-revision summary reflects the actual highest-risk changes.
- [ ] In a later round, each comment is marked closed, residual, reopened, new, or execution-only.
- [ ] Every promise from the prior response is implemented, explicitly superseded, or disclosed as unresolved.
- [ ] The current letter summarizes incremental changes rather than repeating the prior round wholesale.

## Truthfulness of claimed changes

- [ ] Every statement using `we revised`, `we added`, `we removed`, or `we show` is verified in the supplied manuscript or supplement.
- [ ] Planned work is not described as completed.
- [ ] Section, page, line, proposition, figure, table, appendix, and supplement references are accurate in the final response version.
- [ ] Short quoted revisions match the manuscript exactly.

## Model and analytical integrity

- [ ] Information, timing, observability, commitment, enforcement, and decision rights are internally consistent.
- [ ] New assumptions appear in the model, analysis, proofs, and notation consistently.
- [ ] Deleted mechanisms and obsolete notation have been removed everywhere.
- [ ] New equilibria, refinements, comparative statics, welfare claims, and extensions have been independently checked.
- [ ] The response distinguishes baseline results, extensions, and supplementary findings.
- [ ] “Robust” claims specify what survives and what changes.
- [ ] Mechanical policy consequences are not mislabeled as strategic instruments.
- [ ] Endogenous decision ownership is consistent with timing, observability, and feasibility.
- [ ] Full-information or first-best benchmarks support every efficiency or welfare comparison.

## Institutional and implementation claims

- [ ] Observed practices are separated from proposed mechanisms.
- [ ] Institutional examples and policy descriptions are sourced and current when required.
- [ ] Proposed mechanisms identify the decision maker, observability, enforcement, and implementation boundary.
- [ ] Stylized-model implications are not presented as field-validated prescriptions.
- [ ] Time-sensitive platform practices and policy names have been reverified for the current round.

## Literature and contribution

- [ ] Every new citation exists, is verified, and appears in the correct bibliography.
- [ ] Closest-paper comparisons use mechanism-level dimensions.
- [ ] Claims of generalization or nesting identify and verify the exact restriction.
- [ ] The abstract, introduction, literature review, conclusion, and response letter state the same revised contribution.
- [ ] Removed or weakened contributions no longer appear elsewhere.
- [ ] The decisive distinction from the closest paper appears in the manuscript, not only in the response letter.
- [ ] `If the policy works` and `how, when, or by whom it should be used` contributions are not conflated.
- [ ] Result-level literature comparisons state whether conclusions agree, reverse, or qualify one another and explain the mechanism rather than relying on a citation or setting difference.

## Stakeholder and evaluative language

- [ ] `Optimal` identifies the optimizing actor and objective.
- [ ] `Preferred` identifies the selection criterion.
- [ ] `Dominates` identifies the alternatives, metric, and parameter region.
- [ ] `Necessary` is logically established rather than observed in examples.
- [ ] `Win-win` includes every named stakeholder and the relevant benchmark.
- [ ] Separation or identification is not used as a synonym for positive consumer surplus or efficiency.
- [ ] Policy rankings identify the objective; profit, success probability, volume, and consumer surplus are audited separately.

## Presentation

- [ ] Comments and responses are visually distinguishable without relying only on color.
- [ ] Equations, symbols, references, URLs, figures, and screenshots render correctly.
- [ ] Repeated explanations are consistent but tailored to the recipient's concern.
- [ ] The response is concise enough that the action and evidence are easy to locate.
- [ ] Typographical and grammatical corrections are complete across all files.
- [ ] Terminology replacements are global and identify the economic construct and decision owner.
- [ ] No drafting artifacts remain: `XXX`, TODOs, “copy and paste,” author notes, tentative alternatives, or mismatched table and proposition numbers.
- [ ] Every proposition and corollary has a substantive discussion that states its role relative to the preceding benchmark or result; reverse mappings are identified as such rather than presented as new mechanisms.
- [ ] Every revised figure panel is tied to the supporting proposition or corollary, and the surrounding prose explains axis movements, thresholds, cross-panel contrasts, and the economic consequence.
- [ ] Changes to a proposition have been propagated to its paired results, literature comparison, figures, captions, and downstream discussion.

## Release gate

Do not label the package ready if any critical model claim, cited fact, manuscript location, or promised revision remains unverified. Produce a short unresolved-items list with the owner and required evidence.
