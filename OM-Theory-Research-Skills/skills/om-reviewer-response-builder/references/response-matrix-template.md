# Response matrix template

Maintain this matrix internally even if the final deliverable is a prose letter.

| ID | Round lineage | Reviewer comment or concern | Root risk | Priority | Disposition | Required analysis or evidence | Manuscript action | Verified location | Status | Author decision needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AE-1 | New / residual from R1-AE-2 / reopened / execution-only | Verbatim or concise internal summary | Internal consistency / analysis / fit / contribution / welfare / exposition | Critical / structural / strengthening / editorial | Accept / remove / rebuild / extend / clarify / partial / decline | Proof, computation, model comparison, source, literature, or scope argument | Exact edit | Section; page/line only when stable | Planned / in progress / verified / blocked | Yes/No and question |

## Prior-promise ledger

## Objective and stakeholder ledger

Use when a response compares policies or claims protection, superiority, or a win-win:

| Policy or regime | Decision owner | Objective | Separation or identification | Firm profit | Consumer surplus | Success or volume | Total surplus if defined | Safe conclusion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Do not collapse identification, campaign success, firm profit, and consumer welfare into one measure.

## Cross-reviewer synthesis

Track whether one change answers several comments:

| Revision package | Comments addressed | Why one change resolves the common root issue | Recipient-specific emphasis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Do not merge comments in the final letter merely because they share one manuscript change. Each reviewer should still receive an independently understandable response.

## Status definitions

- **Planned:** the action is chosen but not implemented.
- **In progress:** analysis or writing exists but has not been reconciled.
- **Verified:** the result and claimed manuscript change have been checked.
- **Blocked:** author judgment, new data, computation, citation verification, or stable pagination is missing.

The response letter may say “we have revised” only for verified items.
