|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use `NESTED`, `PARTIALLY NESTED`, `NONNESTED`, or `UNVERIFIED`. Use `generalizes` or `subsumes` only for `NESTED` relationships that recover the relevant result.

## Contribution ledger

| Proposed contribution | Relative to | Strongest overlap | Structural difference | Information-stage, decision-right, or instrument change | Changed payoff/constraint/preference geometry | Mechanism consequence | Verified result | Strength | Approved wording |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use one of these strength labels: `strong`, `moderate`, `incremental`, `setting-only`, or `unverified`.

## Positioning paragraph pattern

1. Define the literature stream and its central decision problem.
2. Identify the closest paper or small cluster and state what it endogenizes.
3. State the focal paper's exact structural difference.
4. Explain why that difference changes the mechanism or equilibrium.
5. State the verified new result and its boundary.

## Stream architecture

| Stream | Central decision problem | Mechanism clusters | Closest paper(s) | Focal difference | Result enabled | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

End the review with a cross-stream synthesis that explains why linking the streams changes the equilibrium question; do not merely repeat the paper's setting.

Avoid paper-by-paper catalogues unless the user specifically requests a comprehensive review.
